PHOTOGRAPHS GO IN THIS FOLDER
=============================

Two steps to add an image:

  1. Put the file here, in public/images/
  2. Open  src/data/images.js  and fill in that image's entry

No page files need editing. Until step 2 is done, the page shows
a grey placeholder — nothing breaks.


WHAT IS NEEDED
--------------

  dr-hitesh-portrait.jpg    Homepage hero. Portrait, upright.
                            1200 x 1500 px.
                            The most important image on the site.

  trichoscopy.jpg           Dermatoscope or trichoscope in use.
                            1200 x 900 px. Landscape.
                            Clinical, not posed.

  consultation.jpg          Consultation in progress.
                            1200 x 750 px. Landscape.

  dr-hitesh-about.jpg       Portrait for the About section.
                            1200 x 1500 px.
                            Can be the same file as the hero.

  clinic-narela.jpg         Optional. 1200 x 800 px.
  clinic-rohini.jpg         Optional. 1200 x 800 px.


BEFORE YOU UPLOAD
-----------------

Compress every photograph. A camera JPEG is 4-8 MB and will undo
the work done on page speed. Compressed, each should be
150-300 KB with no visible difference.

Use https://squoosh.app — free, runs in the browser, nothing is
uploaded anywhere. Open the file, set quality to about 80, check
it still looks right, download.

Resize to the pixel dimensions listed above. Larger files are
not sharper on screen; they are just slower.

Rename to lowercase with hyphens. No spaces, no capitals, no
brackets. "dr-hitesh-portrait.jpg", not "DR HITESH (1).JPG".


PHOTOGRAPHY NOTES
-----------------

Use real photographs of Dr Hitesh and the actual clinics.

Do not use stock images of models with flawless skin. Every
competitor site does this and it is the single fastest way to
lose credibility with a patient who is comparing options.

Any clinical or treatment imagery should show Indian skin tones.

Patient photographs need written consent before publication,
separately from any consent given for the clinical record.
